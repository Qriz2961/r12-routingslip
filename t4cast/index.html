Zone dataset0 zones▶
One row per city or municipality. Population, households, employment and enrolment drive trip generation; income drives car ownership; land area sets the intrazonal trip length.
Corridor network0 links▶
One row per road section between two zones. Links are two-way; capacity is per direction. Zones with no link are still modelled, but only for intrazonal travel.
Model parameters. Every coefficient used by the four-step simulation, with the formula it appears in. Defaults are indicative planning values — replace them with locally calibrated figures where you have them.
Mode characteristics0 modes▶
Fare, speed and comfort attributes feed the logit mode-choice utility. Occupancy and PCU convert person trips into vehicles for assignment; seats and CO₂ drive fleet sizing and the emissions estimate.
